Question 1: Labels and positions (1 point(s))

A table has row labels [104, 101, 107, 103, 109], in that order. Which labels are selected by table.iloc[1:3]?

A. 101 and 103

B. 104, 101, and 107

C. 101 and 107 (Correct answer)

D. 1, 2, and 3

Rationale: iloc selects positions 1 and 2 and excludes position 3. The labels at those positions are 101 and 107.

Question 2: Combining conditions (1 point(s))

Which mask selects movie records rated at least 8 AND with a production budget below 50,000,000?

A. (movies['IMDB Rating'] >= 8) & (movies['Production Budget'] < 50000000) (Correct answer)

B. (movies['IMDB Rating'] >= 8) | (movies['Production Budget'] < 50000000)

C. (movies['IMDB Rating'] >= 8) and (movies['Production Budget'] < 50000000)

D. (movies['IMDB Rating'] > 8) & (movies['Production Budget'] <= 50000000)

Rationale: The required boundaries are >= 8 and < 50000000. Parentheses and & combine the two elementwise comparisons.

Question 3: Keeping a renamed result (1 point(s))

A DataFrame named selected_movies contains 'gross_minus_budget_millions'. You run report = selected_movies.rename(columns={'gross_minus_budget_millions': 'gross_budget_gap_millions'}). Which statement is correct?

A. Both tables now use the new column name.

B. report is None because rename always changes the original table.

C. Neither table uses the new column name until the notebook is saved.

D. report uses the new column name, while selected_movies keeps the old name. (Correct answer)

Rationale: rename returns a new DataFrame by default. Assigning its result keeps the renamed table without renaming the original.

Question 4: Choosing a denominator (1 point(s))

A movie table has 100 records, of which 10 have missing ratings. Of the 90 records with observed ratings, 18 have ratings of at least 8. What proportion of records with observed ratings have a rating of at least 8?

A. 18 / 100 = 18%

B. 18 / 90 = 20% (Correct answer)

C. 90 / 100 = 90%

D. 18 / 10 = 180%

Rationale: The question conditions on an observed rating, so the denominator is 90, not all 100 records.
